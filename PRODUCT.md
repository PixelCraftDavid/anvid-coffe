# Anvid Coffe

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Customers who buy and taste Anvid Coffe, primarily using their phones.

## Product Purpose
A premium scratch-and-reveal experience where a customer may win a coffee and can optionally leave an honest review about the coffee they tasted.

## Brand Commitments
The user supplied the exact name “Anvid Coffe”. They requested premium, elegant, clean, polished design with fluid motion and references to motion.dev, 21st.dev, Skiper UI, animmasterlib.dev and horizonx.so.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The current experience is an explicitly labeled interactive demonstration. It supports actual canvas scratching, an accessible reveal button, winning and nonwinning outcomes, a downloadable demonstration pass, local persistence and an optional coffee review. Opinions concern taste, aroma, preparation and service, never the gift. A review never affects eligibility or the result.

## Open Decisions
Real purchase validation, prize allocation, redemption, promotion rules, campaign dates, actual café products, real review destination and server storage have not been supplied. No fictitious social proof, location or reviews are used.

## Confirmed deployment preferences
Only free plans and services, without enabling paid billing. The intended setup order is GitHub, Vercel, then Firebase Spark; the owner has a GitHub account but no project setup has been completed. Requested database records are coupons (identifier, win/no-win result and coupon date/time) and coffee reviews (one 1–5 star rating, comment and date/time). Database integration remains pending platform setup.
