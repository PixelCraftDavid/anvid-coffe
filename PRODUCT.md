# Anvid Coffe

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Customers who buy and taste Anvid Coffe, primarily using their phones.

## Product Purpose
A premium scratch-and-reveal experience where a customer may win a coffee and can optionally leave an honest review about the coffee they tasted.

## Brand Commitments
The user supplied the exact name “Anvid Coffe”. They requested premium, elegant, clean, polished design with fluid motion and references to motion.dev, 21st.dev, Skiper UI, animmasterlib.dev and horizonx.so.

## Capabilities and Constraints
The experience supports canvas scratching, an accessible reveal button, winning and nonwinning outcomes, a downloadable coupon, and optional coffee reviews. Vercel's server API and Firebase Firestore replace the old local-only demonstration. Results are decided on the server with at most one global winner per UTC−6 day, protected by a transaction. Opinions concern the coffee, never the gift. A review never affects eligibility or the result.

## Open Decisions
Authenticated customer identity, campaign dates, coupon expiration and actual café products have not been supplied. Purchase-gated play uses one fixed printed QR, a short pending identifier and explicit staff approval, backed by one browser-bound attempt per recorded coffee; the browser cookie restores the same result and does not establish identity. The owner requested a private staff panel and single-use redemption, implemented with a shared server-side password and atomic Firestore redemption. No fictitious social proof, location or reviews are used.

## Purchase passes and voxel QR — 24 October 2026

The owner approved separate purchase and redemption QR flows. Each new manually registered coffee produces a 192-bit random bearer pass, created atomically with the sale. Staff recover the same passes from sale history; idempotent sale retries never issue more. Public play now requires a valid pass. Opening a pass only checks it; reveal atomically checks the sale, consumes the pass, creates its coupon and reserves any daily prize. A used pass restores only to its original browser, including after a date change. Another paid coffee permits another attempt in that same browser, replacing the former browser/day limit. Voids invalidate pending passes; already issued outcomes and prizes remain intact. No pass expiry was invented. Passes can be transferred before first use and are not customer identity verification. Older sales are not backfilled with passes. Existing winning coupons remain valid.

Purchase URLs carry their token in a fragment, removed after capture and retained in memory/session storage; API submissions use the request body. Public pages do not enumerate passes. The signed participant cookie restores a last-result pointer. Staff pass status refreshes on actual coupon events. Reviews remain voluntary and independently accessible.

The owner's tree/QR reference pins the revised artwork: a flat QR unfolds into a square voxel mug, coffee/foam, squared handle, steam blocks, beans and small plants on a platform. This replaces the earlier QR printed on a solid cube. Shared positional colors connect coffee/caramel/cream/sage/olive in both views. A cached QR texture and fewer than 250 voxels animate in one canvas for 280 ms; there is no idle renderer or new 3D dependency. Keyboard/reduced-motion changes are immediate. Scanning and downloads use the true flat matrix with its quiet zone; perspective artwork is not promised readable. Canjes has a visible example and a real isolated TEST generator. The same component serves actual purchase passes and winning redemption coupons. Existing free-plan quotas apply.

The one daily winner among the first 15 valid attempts, voluntary reviews, one-use prize redemption and existing animation world remain unchanged. Legacy individual passes stay compatible.
